# Customer Segmentation Project

## Objective
Segment customers based on behavior and demographics and identify groups that can be targeted differently.

## Tools
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- Excel

## Dataset
This project uses **300 synthetic customer records** created for learning and portfolio purposes.

## Methodology
K-Means clustering was applied to:
- Annual Income
- Spending Score
- Purchase Frequency

The features were standardized before clustering.

## Customer Segments
1. **High-Value Customers** — strong spending and/or purchase activity.
2. **Regular Customers** — consistent customers with moderate activity.
3. **Potential Customers** — customers who have room to increase engagement.
4. **Low-Value Customers** — lower spending and/or lower purchase activity.

## Key Insights
- High-Value Customers generates the highest total revenue at ₹71,330.
- Potential Customers has the highest average spending score (75.3/100).
- Delhi is the highest-revenue city with approximately ₹39,019 in revenue.
- Online is the highest-revenue purchasing channel.
- High-value customers should receive loyalty rewards, personalized offers and retention campaigns.
- Potential customers should be targeted with personalized recommendations and first/next-purchase incentives.
- Low-value customers can be reached with low-cost campaigns and reactivation offers.

## Files

## Business Recommendations
- Reward high-value customers with loyalty benefits and personalized offers.
- Encourage potential customers with targeted recommendations and incentives.
- Retain regular customers through personalized campaigns.
- Use cost-efficient reactivation campaigns for low-value customers.
